Privacy Policy

PMHNP Prep · Edict Digital · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Summary: PMHNP Prep does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your study progress stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app works entirely offline. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app and we do not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your answers, readiness score, flagged questions, flashcard schedule (including cards made from questions you missed), mock exam and case results, exam date, checklist and preferences are stored locally on your device, and a small summary is shared with the app's own widgets, notifications and Live Activity through your device's keychain. This data may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud) according to your settings; those backups are handled by Apple, not by us. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings → Reset all progress or by deleting the app.

Listen mode and search

Listen mode uses the speech voices built into your device; the text is read on your iPhone and never sent anywhere. To let you find drugs, lessons and cases from iOS search, the app adds their titles to your device's on-device Spotlight index.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether your account has an active PMHNP Prep Pro entitlement. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device (a daily board question, streak and exam-countdown reminders). Answers you give inside a notification or widget stay on your device. No data is sent to us or to a push notification service.

Children

PMHNP Prep is an educational app for nursing professionals and students. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

PMHNP Prep is a study tool for certification exam preparation. It is not medical advice and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment. PMHNP Prep is not affiliated with or endorsed by ANCC, AANPCB/NPCB or any certifying body.
